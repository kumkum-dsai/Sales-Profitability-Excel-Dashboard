# Sales and Profitability Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive, dark-themed Excel dashboard that analyses sales and profitability across products, regions, and discount levels. It turns 10,194 rows of order data into KPIs, charts, and slicer-based filtering, so a business user can see what drives profit and what destroys it.

## Objective
To find out where the business makes and loses money, and to present the answer in one clear, interactive dashboard.

## Dataset
- 10,194 order line records
- Fields cover orders, products (category and sub-category), regions, discounts, sales, profit, quantity, shipping, and returns

## Key KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | 23,26,534 |
| Total Profit | 2,92,297 |
| Profit Margin | 12.6% |
| Unique Orders | 5,111 |
| Total Quantity | 38,654 |
| Average Order Value | 455.20 |
| Average Shipping Days | 4.0 |
| Order Return Rate | 5.8% |

## Key Findings
- **Overall performance:** The business earned a 12.6% profit margin on 2.33M of sales.
- **Growth:** Sales grew 21.4% from 2025 to 2026.
- **Best sub-category:** Copiers, with 56.1K profit.
- **Worst sub-category:** Tables, with a loss of 17.8K.
- **Loss-making sub-categories:** Supplies, Bookcases, and Tables all lose money.
- **Category margins:** Furniture has the lowest margin at 2.6%, compared with 17.4% for Technology.
- **Regional margins:** They range from 7.9% in Central to 15.0% in West.
- **Discounting:** Medium and high discount lines made a loss of 136K on 365K of sales, so heavy discounting is destroying value.
- **Returns:** 5.8% of orders were returned.

## Recommendations
- Cap or review medium and high discounts, especially on Furniture.
- Review pricing and costs for Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies.
- Investigate why Central lags West by about 7 percentage points in margin.

## Tools and Skills Used
